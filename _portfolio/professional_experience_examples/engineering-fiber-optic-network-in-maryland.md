**Organization:** ThinkBig Networks  
**My role:** GIS systems engineer

At ThinkBig Networks, I used GIS to support the planning, engineering, and documentation of fiber optic networks in Maryland. My responsibilities connected network design with construction information and the maps used to build and maintain the system. The work included both day-to-day engineering support and improvements to how recurring GIS products were created.

In Charles, Kent, Harford and Queen Anne's County, I engineered and managed fiber network construction work that supported broadband access for more than 2,000 customers. 

I standardized geoprocessing, georeferencing, and digitizing procedures used in the engineering work. Those are recurring tasks in translating source information into usable network data. A consistent approach made them easier to repeat across the project and helped the GIS work support the team's engineering and construction needs.

One of my clearest process improvements involved map production. As-Built and construction mapbooks had previously required substantial manual work for mapping out as-built stationing. I led the automation of more than 400 mapbooks, reducing production time by 75 percent. The change improved the process for producing the mapping documents used by the project team.

The distinction between construction and As-Built information was important to the role. Construction maps supported planned work, while As-Built documentation recorded the network that had been installed. If customers required any changes to either, then I would produce those changes to meet customer needs.

The database work and the map production work were closely connected: the usefulness of a mapbook depended on the underlying network records, and changes to those records needed to be reflected in internal asset management.
