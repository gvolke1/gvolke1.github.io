**Organization:** Blackwater Environmental Group  
**My role:** Environmental planner

I supported the Chesapeake Bay Crossing Study through demographic research, cartography, interactive mapping, and public engagement. As an environmental planner at Blackwater Environmental Group, I worked on materials that helped the project team examine community conditions and communicate information about a major transportation study.

The assignment brought together technical analysis and public-facing work. The Tier 2 NEPA study considered alternatives for improving travel across the Chesapeake Bay. My role included researching the communities in the study area, developing mapping products, and helping organize and support engagement activities where residents could learn about the project and share their perspectives.

I also created cartographic products and interactive maps in ArcGIS Online, integrating spatial datasets and visualizing input from more than 2,000 public survey respondents. I developed a form for bridge-survey data and helped turn the collected information into mapping products that could support discussion. Connecting the responses to a geographic view made the public input available in a form the planning team could explore.

My responsibilities extended to community engagement materials and public meetings. I helped organize engagement activities, prepared information for a public audience, and interacted directly with community members and stakeholders. Those responsibilities required communicating technical material in plain language for broader conversation about a consequential infrastructure project.
