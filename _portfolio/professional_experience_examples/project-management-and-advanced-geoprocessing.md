**Organization:** ThinkBig Networks  
**My role:** GIS systems engineer and project coordinator

At ThinkBig Networks, I worked across multiple fiber projects using geoprocessing, data integration, and operational reporting. My contribution included improving recurring GIS procedures and connecting spatial information with the work order records used to manage activity in the field.

The role involved more than producing an individual map. Network projects generated information about planned work, completed work, assets, customers, and construction progress. My responsibilities included organizing spatial data and finding practical ways to connect it to the records used by the wider team.

I developed Scheduled Work Order and Completed Work Order reports by combining GIS with other datasets. That integration supported an 80 percent increase in processed work orders. The improvement came from bringing information into a reporting workflow that could better support the operational tasks the team needed to carry out.

I also performed quality-control checks on geospatial data and related records, identifying discrepancies affecting information such as billing, easements, customers, and cadastral data. These checks were important to the reporting work because the value of a combined dataset depends on the reliability of the records being brought together.

Across my engineering responsibilities, I standardized geoprocessing, georeferencing, and digitizing procedures. Those improvements addressed recurring work in creating and maintaining spatial information. Establishing a consistent approach helped connect the GIS tasks with the engineering and construction needs of the projects I supported.

The work required keeping both the individual task and the broader delivery process in view. A geoprocessing result needed to fit the records and reports used by colleagues. A work order report needed information from more than one source. My contribution was to make those connections useful and to improve the repeated steps that supported them.
