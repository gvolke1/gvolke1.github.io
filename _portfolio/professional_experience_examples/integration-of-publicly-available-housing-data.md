**Organization:** ThinkBig Networks  
**My role:** GIS systems engineer

I incorporated publicly available Maryland cadastral information into fiber network planning at ThinkBig Networks. The work connected parcel and property information with the spatial data used to plan network expansion, helping the team consider the land and property context surrounding proposed infrastructure.

I automated the process of updating our infrastructure footprint in Miss Utility’s 811 system. Previously, each line segment had to be drawn manually through the website’s interface. This made recurring submissions time-consuming and required recreating geographic information we already maintained in GIS.

I contacted Miss Utility to identify a faster submission method and coordinated an alternative that accepted our engineered GIS segments directly. Using QGIS, I prepared the line segments and established an automated weekly upload workflow to update our infrastructure footprint in their backend system.

This replaced repetitive drawing in the web interface with a repeatable process using our existing GIS data. It reduced manual data entry and made ongoing infrastructure updates easier to maintain. Identifying the solution required both technical work and direct communication with the service provider to understand the available submission options.
