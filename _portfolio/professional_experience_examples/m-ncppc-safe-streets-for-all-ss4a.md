**Organization:** Blackwater Environmental Group  
**My role:** Environmental planner and GIS analyst

I conducted environmental screening and GIS analysis for 23 transportation safety project areas within the M-NCPPC Safe Streets for All initiative. My work helped the planning team identify natural and cultural resource considerations associated with proposed improvements around parks and recreational facilities.

The project combined a public-safety objective with the need to understand conditions at individual sites. A pedestrian or bicycle improvement could intersect with environmental resources that required further attention during planning. Evaluating those relationships across multiple project areas called for a consistent approach to reviewing locations, identifying resources, and communicating the findings.

I carried out desktop reviews of sensitive species information, waterways, wetlands, habitats, and historical resources. Using GIS, I assessed how project areas related to the available resource layers. I calculated polygon overlap areas to help describe the spatial relationships in measurable terms and produced maps and reports that brought those findings together.

The analysis supported the team's consideration of environmental review topics, including Section 106 and Section 4(f). My role was to prepare the geographic and descriptive information needed for that work. The maps and summaries gave project managers a way to see where a proposed improvement encountered a resource consideration and where additional review might be needed.

The multi-site nature of the assignment made consistency especially useful. Applying spatial analysis across 23 areas helped organize the review into a form the team could compare. The GIS calculations supplied a common analytical basis, while the written material explained the conditions associated with each location. That combination connected the technical overlay work to the decisions being considered by planners.

My contribution helped bring environmental considerations into the discussion of safety improvements. It supported evaluation of project locations and informed the team's efforts to minimize impacts as proposals developed. The work gave decision-makers site-specific evidence to consider alongside the transportation goals of the initiative.
