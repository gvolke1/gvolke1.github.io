**Organization:** Blackwater Environmental Group  
**My role:** Environmental planner and GIS analyst

I supported environmental review for SHA road and bridge projects, including Programmatic Categorical Exclusions and minor PCEs in SHA Districts 3, 4, and 6. My work combined GIS analysis, demographic research, agency coordination, and written environmental documentation to give project teams a clearer picture of the conditions associated with proposed improvements.

The assignments required attention to the geographic setting of each project. Road maintenance and bridge improvements could involve natural habitats, cultural resources, scenic considerations, and nearby communities. My role was to assemble and analyze relevant information and present it in the maps and summaries used during review.

I generated GIS data and shapefiles to evaluate project areas in relation to those resources. I also contributed demographic analyses, land-use assessments, and environmental justice screening. These activities brought community and environmental information into the same planning process, helping the team understand the different considerations associated with a location.

I worked with agencies, including the US Fish and Wildlife Service, on information relevant to endangered species and habitat review. That coordination connected the geographic analysis with the organizations responsible for particular resources. I prepared material to support review rather than treating a completed map as the end of the task.

My broader SHA portfolio included maps and analytical summaries for more than 200 state highway projects across six Maryland counties. The PCE assignments formed part of that larger body of environmental planning work. Handling a substantial project portfolio required translating site-specific information into documentation that project managers and reviewers could use consistently.

The contribution of this work was a clearer analytical basis for considering environmental and community conditions as projects developed. Maps located the relevant features, demographic analysis described nearby populations, and the written summaries connected those findings to the project being reviewed. Each piece helped the team understand what the available information showed and what needed consideration in the next stage.
