**Organization:** Blackwater Environmental Group  
**My role:** Environmental planner and correspondence coordinator

I supported the Key Bridge Replacement Project as an environmental planner, with responsibility for coordinating correspondence among project sponsors, government agencies, and regulatory officials. My work focused on making communication more consistent and supporting the environmental review process during an expedited, highly visible infrastructure effort.

The project required coordination across organizations with different responsibilities. Communication had to support work involving NEPA, Section 106, and Section 4(f), while keeping the project team connected with the officials and stakeholders involved in review. My contribution was to organize that correspondence and make the process clearer for the people using it.

I led the development of communication templates, schedules, and procedures. Standardizing those elements addressed a practical process problem: when many organizations need to exchange information, inconsistent correspondence can make coordination harder to manage. A common structure helped the team prepare communications in a consistent way and approach the recurring work with clearer expectations.

I coordinated closely with project sponsors and regulatory officials as the review work progressed. This involved managing the correspondence process and supporting communication between the parties responsible for different aspects of the project. The work required careful writing, attention to the purpose of each communication, and an understanding of how a document fit within the wider environmental planning effort.

The process improvements supported the team's work toward regulatory milestones on an expedited schedule. My role contributed to the clarity and organization of that effort, alongside the technical and review responsibilities of the agencies and other project participants. The deliverable was a more consistent approach to coordination during a period when dependable communication was particularly important.

This experience strengthened my ability to contribute where technical projects depend on work across organizational boundaries. I can turn recurring communication needs into practical templates and procedures, write for multiple audiences, and support a team through a demanding coordination process. Those capabilities are useful in GIS and data projects as well: successful delivery depends on clear communication about responsibilities, information needs, and the work being passed between people.

**Skills demonstrated:** Interagency correspondence; Communication templates; Coordination schedules; Regulatory review support; Process standardization.

**Tools and working materials:** Correspondence templates; Communication schedules; Environmental review documentation.
