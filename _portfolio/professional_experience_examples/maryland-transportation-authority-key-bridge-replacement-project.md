**Organization:** Blackwater Environmental Group  
**My role:** Environmental planner and agency coordinator

I supported the [Key Bridge Replacement Project] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Scott_Key_Bridge_collapse) as an environmental planner, with responsibility for coordinating correspondence among project sponsors, government agencies, and regulatory officials. My work focused on making communication more consistent and supporting the environmental review process during an expedited, highly visible infrastructure effort.

The project required coordination across organizations with different responsibilities. Communication had to support work involving NEPA, Section 106, and Section 4(f), while keeping the project team connected with the officials and stakeholders involved in review. My contribution was to organize that correspondence and make the process clearer for the people using it.

Working under pressure with tight deadlines during an emergency infrastructure effort that received [global attention](https://www.cnn.com/2024/03/29/us/baltimore-port-francis-scott-key-bridge), my role focused on consolidating handwritten and typed notes from multiple participants into formal records of meetings between government agencies and project stakeholders.

Preparing records for meetings I had not attended required careful interpretation, comparison, and organization of the source notes. I brought this material into a consistent, readable format for later review, helping participants maintain a shared understanding of discussions as the project advanced on an expedited schedule.
