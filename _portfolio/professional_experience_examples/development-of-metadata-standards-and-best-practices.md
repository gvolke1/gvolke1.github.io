**Organization:** ThinkBig Networks  
**My role:** GIS systems engineer

I established internal metadata standards for network points and developed GIS data management practices at ThinkBig Networks. The work supported a more consistent approach to documenting the spatial information used in fiber planning, construction, and asset management.

My wider role involved maintaining records for network assets, construction updates, splicing, customers, and equipment. That experience made the documentation of the data an operational concern. Information needed to remain understandable and consistent as it moved through the different activities associated with network delivery.

I developed internal standards for describing network points and guidance for managing the GIS data. These provided a common basis for the team's records and helped standardize recurring work, reducing inconsistent builds that happened before standardization. 

This work complemented the quality-control responsibilities I carried elsewhere in the role. Checking a dataset can identify discrepancies at a particular point in time. Consistent metadata and management practices also support the ongoing work of creating, updating, and interpreting records. Both activities contributed to the reliability of the GIS information.

The value of the project was a clearer, more consistent foundation for using network data across project phases. I approached documentation as part of the working system: anyone should be able to pickup the documentation and know what to do. 

I bring that attention to the structure and usability of data to other GIS and analytical work. I can help a team develop practical documentation, improve recurring procedures, and maintain information that remains useful beyond its initial creation. The result is work that is easier for colleagues to understand and continue using as projects develop.
