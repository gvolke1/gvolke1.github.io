**Organization:** New Light Technologies  
**My role:** Power BI developer and documentation author

I developed an interactive Power BI organizational chart to help FEMA Risk MAP users explore reporting relationships and staffing information. The tool allowed users to see who reported to whom and filter personnel by division, branch, office location, and other organizational attributes. I also wrote step-by-step instructions so managers could refresh it when staffing or reporting relationships changed.

The need extended beyond displaying an organizational hierarchy. Users needed to find relevant people and understand the part of the organization associated with a staffing or capacity question. Filtering made it possible to narrow the view to a division, branch, or location and examine that information without relying on a single static chart.

My work translated organizational information into an interactive format designed for management using Lucid. The resulting chart supported questions about reporting relationships, vacancies, and the distribution of personnel. Presenting those attributes together gave users a way to explore the organization through the dimensions relevant to their responsibilities.

Maintaining the tool was an equally important part of the assignment. Employees change roles, vacancies open or close, and reporting lines move. An organizational chart needs a repeatable update process if it is going to remain useful after the initial build. I produced documentation explaining how to refresh the tool when those underlying details changed.

I wrote the instructions for nontechnical FEMA managers. That required treating the maintenance workflow as part of the deliverable and explaining the work in a way that the people responsible for future updates could follow. The documentation supported a handoff in which the application could continue to be maintained as organizational information evolved.
