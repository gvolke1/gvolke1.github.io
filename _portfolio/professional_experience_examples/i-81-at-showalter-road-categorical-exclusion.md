**Organization:** Blackwater Environmental Group  
**My role:** Environmental planner

I contributed to the Categorical Exclusion document for the I-81 project at Showalter Road. My work included demographic analysis, environmental justice screening, project documentation, and public meeting materials. The assignment required bringing community information into the review of a proposed transportation improvement and explaining it clearly to the audiences involved.

I used demographic information and environmental justice screening tools to examine populations in the project area. That analysis supported consideration of potential effects on vulnerable communities. The purpose was to provide project-specific information that could inform the broader environmental review and help the team understand the community context of the proposal.

I worked with stakeholders on the project background, impact assessment material, and discussion of mitigation. These topics needed to be connected within the Categorical Exclusion document so readers could understand the proposed work and the issues being considered.
