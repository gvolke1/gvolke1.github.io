**Organization:** Blackwater Environmental Group  
**My role:** Environmental planner and program support

I supported MDOT SHA's Transportation Alternatives and Recreational Trails work through field inspections, invoice review, project coordination, and improvements to internal procedures. The assignment connected the physical progress of transportation projects with the documentation needed to manage state and federal grant resources.

My responsibilities included inspecting trail and bridge projects and reviewing the condition and progress of work in the field. Those activities provided a practical view of the infrastructure behind the program records. I worked with project sponsors to follow progress and support the information exchange associated with program administration.

I reviewed invoices covering project materials and labor. This work required careful attention to the information submitted for payment and its relationship to the project being supported. It formed part of the program's oversight process.

A process-improvement component of the role involved developing clearer workflow documentation and strengthening internal quality control. The existing processes presented opportunities to organize recurring tasks more effectively. I helped document those tasks and improve how the work was carried out. 

My responsibilities spanned physical inspections, construction activities, and detailed invoice reviews of materials, labor, and costs. I also coordinated critical information exchange among project sponsors and participants.
