Welcome to Griff Volker's professional website. Robert “Griff” Volker is a geospatial analyst, developer, and data professional with experience building GIS applications, automated data pipelines, interactive dashboards, and spatial decision-support tools for emergency management, public safety, infrastructure, and government clients. At New Light Technologies, he has supported projects involving disaster-risk analysis, fire and emergency response optimization, 911 operations, and web-based mapping. His work includes developing Python-based ETL workflows, improving the integration of Census and hazard data within H3 spatial grids, structuring PostGIS databases, and contributing to interactive applications built with JavaScript, Leaflet, HTML, CSS, R, and ArcGIS.

His developer experience at NLT also includes creating lightweight web mapping applications, integrating ArcGIS Feature Services, automating data ingestion, and developing the technical infrastructure needed to turn complex datasets into accessible decision-making tools. Griff has also contributed to FEMA-related work, applying his experience with hazard data, spatial analysis, application development, and emergency-management technology to support federal disaster-response needs. Through this work, he has developed the ability to contribute across the full project lifecycle, from identifying and preparing data to building the databases, automated workflows, maps, and interfaces through which that information is delivered.

Griff is completing a Master of Science in Geography and Environmental Systems at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County. His graduate research examines Baltimore’s housing markets and neighborhood change through the development of an alternative housing market typology. Using principal component analysis, clustering, and spatial analysis, his research considers both traditional indicators of neighborhood disinvestment and the conditions that may shape future reinvestment. He also holds a GIS Graduate Certificate from UMBC and is a graduate of Towson University, where he earned bachelor’s degrees in Geography and Environmental Planning and Metropolitan Studies, along with a minor in GIS.

Before joining NLT, Griff developed a broad professional foundation across environmental planning, telecommunications, transportation, and public-sector consulting. At Blackwater Environmental Group, he used GIS to support transportation safety, environmental compliance, equity assessments, and major infrastructure projects, including the Key Bridge Replacement Project and the Chesapeake Bay Crossing Study. Earlier, as a GIS Systems Engineer with ThinkBig Networks, he managed broadband mapping, automated hundreds of construction mapbooks, improved geospatial quality-control processes, and supported network expansion planning across several Maryland counties. He also gained transportation consulting experience with WSP and the Maryland Transit Administration, contributing to fare collection studies, service planning, data reporting, and stakeholder coordination.
