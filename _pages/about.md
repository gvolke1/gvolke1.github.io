Welcome to Griff Volker's professional website. 

Griff Volker is a geospatial analyst, developer, and data professional with experience building GIS applications, automated data pipelines, interactive dashboards, and spatial decision-support tools. His work includes developing Python-based ETL workflows, improving the integration of Census and hazard data within H3 spatial grids, structuring PostGIS databases, and contributing to interactive applications turning complex datasets into accessible decision-making tools. Through this work, he has developed the ability to contribute across the full project lifecycle, from identifying and preparing data to building the databases, automated workflows, maps, and UI/UX design.

Griff is completing a Master of Science in Geography and Environmental Systems at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County. His graduate research examines [Baltimore’s housing typology](https://www.baltimorecity.gov/planning/our-work/maps-data/housing-market-typology) and will develop an alternative approach to identify historically disinvested areas that possess locational advantages for reinvestment. He also holds a GIS Graduate Certificate from UMBC, and is a graduate of Towson University, where he earned bachelor’s degrees in Geography and Environmental Planning and Metropolitan Studies, along with a minor in GIS.

At New Light Technologies, he has supported projects involving disaster-risk analysis, fire and emergency response optimization, 911 operations, and web-based mapping. At Blackwater Environmental Group, he used GIS to support environmental compliance and major infrastructure projects. As a GIS Systems Engineer with ThinkBig Networks, he  improved geospatial quality-control processessupported network expansion planning across several Maryland counties.
