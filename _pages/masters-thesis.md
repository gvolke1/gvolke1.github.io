## Overview

My master's thesis examines Baltimore's housing markets and neighborhood change through the development of an alternative housing market typology. The research studies traditional indicators of neighborhood disinvestment and the conditions that may influence future reinvestment.

## Research Methods

- Principal component analysis
- Cluster analysis
- Spatial statistics and multivariate mapping
- Housing market and neighborhood analysis
- Census, parcel, permit, lending, and land-use data
- Reproducible spatial analysis in R
- GIS data integration and visualization
