### [Master's Thesis Research: Baltimore Housing Typology]({{ '/research/masters-thesis/' | relative_url }})

My master's thesis examines Baltimore's housing markets and neighborhood change through the development of an alternative housing market typology. The research uses principal component analysis, clustering, and spatial analysis to study traditional indicators of neighborhood disinvestment and the conditions that may influence future reinvestment.
